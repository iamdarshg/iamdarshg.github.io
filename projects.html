Custom 6S Battery Management System (BMS)

Power Electronics • Embedded • KiCAD
2024 KiCAD / C++ / STM32 ⭐ 1

A high-performance 6S BMS designed for UAV applications, featuring precise cell balancing, over-current protection, and real-time state-of-charge (SoC) estimation via STM32.

  • KiCAD: Designed custom multi-layer PCB with high-current trace optimization
  • Integrated BQ-series AFE for precise voltage and temperature monitoring
  • STM32 Firmware: Real-time SoC estimation using Extended Kalman Filters
  • Safety Systems: Implemented hardware and software-level protection for over-voltage/current
