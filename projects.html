Code-SDR: Ultra-Wideband Software Defined Radio

FPGA / RF / DSP
Nov 2025 to Q1 2026 Verilog / FPGA / Python Stars: 1

FPGA-accelerated SDR covering 1 MHz to 10 GHz with a real-time processing pipeline. Complete implementation from Verilog HDL to the computer interface with 105 MSPS throughput.

  • LIF-MD6000-6UMG64I FPGA: 105 MSPS real-time processing, under 10 microseconds latency
  • Complete DSP pipeline: NCO mixing, 1024-point FFT, digital downconversion
  • Dual-mode: processed streaming at 750 Mbps or direct ADC streaming at 950 Mbps
  • Production-ready integrations for GNU Radio and Windows ExtIO workflows

Aircraft Diffusion CFD: Generative Aerodynamic Design

Research / AI-ML / CFD
Dec 2025 to ongoing Python / CUDA Stars: 1

GPU-accelerated generative design combining diffusion models with computational fluid dynamics. The training pipeline optimizes aircraft structures for aerodynamic efficiency while preserving structural viability.

  • Diffusion model paired with GPU-accelerated LBM CFD for aerodynamic optimization
  • Progressive grid refinement from 16 cubed to 32 cubed for practical training time
  • Hierarchical structural constraints keep generated designs connected and manufacturable
  • CLI workflow covers training, generation, evaluation, and STL export

ML-Based Rapid RCS Estimation for UAV Stealth

Research / ML regression / Electromagnetics
Research paper draft Python / EM simulation / UAV design Google Drive source

Research paper exploring how trained regression models can estimate UAV radar cross-section from electromagnetic simulation data, enabling faster stealth-design iteration before full-wave validation.

  • Compares dense networks, SVMs, ensembles, volumetric CNNs, transformers, and physics-informed models
  • Defines practical dataset parameters across geometry, frequency, aspect angle, elevation, and polarization
  • Frames ML as a fast screening layer, with final candidates verified by high-fidelity EM simulation
  • Includes metrics, uncertainty guidance, and a 12-week implementation roadmap
