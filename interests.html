05 Interests

The rest of the week.

Four things I do that aren't aerospace. They're the reason I can still sit at a desk for the other five days.

Gym Swimming MUNs Gaming
01 / Gym

The only scoreboard I can't argue with.

I started in the middle of a stretch where most of my day was a screen. Training is the opposite of that: physical, repetitive, and completely uninterested in my opinion.

A missed lift doesn't need a theory. It tells me what to work on next week. Then I go home and sleep properly, which turns out to be most of the programme.

02 / Swimming

An hour with no notifications.

Swimming is the quietest part of my week. Fixed distance, repeated stroke, nothing to check and no way to answer a message.

I usually come out with a better question than the one I went in with. Most days that's more useful than an answer.

03 / MUNs

Learning to make a case in plain language.

Harvard MUN in 2023 and 2024, plus IIT Techfest MUN. The part that stuck wasn't winning the room. It was learning to turn a complicated position into something another person could actually respond to.

I use it every time I have to explain a design decision to someone who wasn't sitting in the CAD file with me.

04 / Gaming

Where I learned to build systems.

Minecraft was the first place I built something with no worksheet telling me the answer. Gather materials, test the idea, break it, try again. The loop felt familiar long before I had a name for it.

I still play Minecraft, Trackmania, and Overwatch. Different kinds of attention: planning a build, finding a faster line, making a call when the round is already going badly.
