A bit about me

If you're looking for someone who treats Maxwell's equations with the same respect as a well-optimized Minecraft redstone circuit, you're in the right place. I don't just build things; I try to understand the "why" behind them—preferably before they catch fire.

From the elegant mathematical beauty of a 3Blue1Brown visualization to the sheer chaotic energy of a Technoblade bedwars match, I find inspiration in the most unlikely places. I believe technical depth doesn't have to be boring, and humor is just a better way to explain complex systems.

Currently, I'm a Grade 11 student in Bengaluru, spending my time between multi-layer PCB design and wondering why my C++ code is throwing a segmentation fault for the fifth time today. When I'm not debugging, I'm probably at the gym or trying to convince people that MUNs are actually just competitive talking.
