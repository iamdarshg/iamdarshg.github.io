Field notes

Where the drawings stopped being enough.

Three moments from this year's work that changed how I build. The images are the actual files, including the ones that make my results look worse.

01 — Geometry

The duct decides everything else.

The fan is the easy part to draw. The duct and the inlet decide the pressure the rotor actually sees, so the assembly gets designed around the airflow — not the other way round. Change a stator row and the rotor that "worked" stops working.

Fusion 360Rotor / stator / duct
02 — Drawing

A drawing is an argument you can check.

Both inlet legs have to reach the rotor face with the same total pressure, or the fan eats a distortion it was never sized for. The centerline and scale bar are there so the claim can be measured by someone who doesn't trust me.

ProjectionSymmetry / mass flow
03 — Test

I don't have one CL number yet. That's the point.

My TSD solver doesn't land on the OpenFOAM RANS points, and the mesh behind those points is still coarse. Until that gap closes, Wing3d reports a lift band across its hyperparameters instead of a single flattering value. This is the plot I'd show first.

About

I'd rather show the plot where my solver is wrong than the one where it looks tidy. That habit is most of this site: the measurement next to the render, the stated uncertainty next to the claim.
